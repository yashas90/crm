## App name

PropNinja CRM

## Short description (Google Play, 80 chars max)

Real estate CRM for agents — leads, follow-ups, and SIM call logging.

## Full description

PropNinja CRM helps real estate sales agents manage leads on the go.

- View and update assigned leads
- Today's follow-up queue
- Place outbound calls via your phone SIM (no VoIP)
- Log call outcomes and schedule next follow-ups
- Secure sign-in with your team account

Requires an active PropNinja CRM organization account. Contact your administrator for access.

## iOS App Review notes

This app opens the native Phone app via `tel:` URLs for outbound calls. It does not use CallKit or VoIP. Call logging is manual/agent-initiated after returning from the dialer.
